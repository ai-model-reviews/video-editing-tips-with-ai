## Multiverse: Four Ways to Change a Video

### Swap the character

**Creative goal:** Replace the person in the original clip with a different character. Same scene, same bottle, new person.

**Inputs:**
- Original video
- A handful of photos of the replacement person, or a saved character

**Actions:**
1. Open the original clip in Multiverse and choose the character swap.
2. Confirm the person in the clip as the subject to replace.
3. Upload the reference photos, or pick a saved character.
4. Add the instruction: "Replace the person with the referenced character. Keep the bottle, the grip, and the studio exactly as they are."
5. Check the model and the token cost on the button, then generate.

> **Character swap:** The original studio clip beside the variation with a new character, made with Multiverse.

**One clip is all this takes. [Run a character swap on yours](https://socialsight.ai/multiverse?clip=2a4f2e12-e831-56c7-b0f3-67fd39a36510?utm_source=github&utm_campaign=how-to-edit-with-ai)**

**What to inspect:** Appearance across frames, facial detail, hands, and how the new character interacts with the bottle.

This result took two generations. The first pass shifted the grip on the bottle, so the second run tightened the instruction to hold the hand position.

### Swap the motion

**Creative goal:** Apply a different motion to the clip.

In Multiverse, "motion" means subject movement. You supply a motion reference clip, and your subject takes on the movement in that reference while the scene stays intact. For this demonstration, the reference is a walk-and-talk: the static studio pose becomes a few steps toward camera with the bottle raised.

**Inputs:**
- Original video
- A motion reference clip showing the movement you want

**Actions:**
1. Open the original clip in Multiverse and choose the motion swap.
2. Upload the motion reference clip.
3. Add the instruction: "Give the subject the movement from the reference clip. Keep the person, the bottle, and the studio unchanged."
4. Check the model and the token cost on the button, then generate.

> **Motion swap:** The original studio clip beside the variation with new subject movement from the walk-and-talk reference, made with Multiverse.

**What to inspect:** Movement, timing, and any visible distortion. Watch the hand and bottle first: held objects are where motion swaps show strain.

## A Real Editing Session With the Agent

An editing brief should identify the video, describe the desired change, and explain what a successful result looks like. Here is the request we gave SocialSight's agent and the edit it produced.

**1. The starting video.**

[Prompt: The original 20 second studio clip]

**2. The exact request.**

> "For the studio water bottle clip, make a 15 second vertical cut for social. The intended result is a clip that opens on the pour and ends holding the bottle hero shot. Preserve the lighting and the logo frame."

**3. What the agent did.**

The agent recut the clip to 9:16, trimmed the opening so the video starts on the pour, tightened the middle beat, and ended on the hero shot of the bottle. It showed the proposed cut before rendering, so the edit was reviewable before a single token was spent.
